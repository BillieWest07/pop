Projector
device used in tandem with a computer to display an image or video on a larger scale
Brightness
The amout of light a projector shows on a screen, usually measured by Lumens
Lumens
The measurement of visible light used. A lighter room would need more lumens to be visible, while a darker room would require less

Projectors

When most are asked to think of a type of technology, the first thing to come to mind is rarely projectors. Of course, it makes sense for most to say laptops, phones, computers, or cameras. That being said, projectors are still a leading device in fields like entertainment, corporations, theatre, cinemas, and education!

In the next couple pages, we dive into the details of this underrated equipment. We follow the history of projectors all the way back to 1600s and what they could be in the future. Next, we disect what goes into the use of this technology, and what it would typically cost. We then tie it all together by outlining how often projectors are actually used in day to day life.

Contrast
The amount of difference between lighter colors and darker colors
Resolution
the amount of pixels used to create an image. The higher the resolution, the more detailed the image.
aspect Ration
the ratio comparing height to width of the image or video being displayed. This would be shown as width:height.
