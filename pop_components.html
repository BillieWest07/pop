Main Components

When you are wanting to setup and use a projector, there are a couple main components that you need!

  1. The Projector Itself
  2. A Power Source
  3. A Video Source
  4. HDMI and Power Cable
  5. Audio Source
  6. A Surface to Project Onto

This is all simple to list, and majority of these items, people already have! Someone could easily find an outlet and the needed cables. Depending ont he device you are viewig this from, yu already have a device ready to conenct to a projector! You could even plug a TV service device into a projector to watch movies. Even the majority of modern projectors come with audio capability! This just leaves the projector, but what should you look for, and what will it cost?
